About

Pune, India

I’m Omkar — a software engineer at Azodha, shipping across mobile and frontend. Off the clock I’m rebuilding my desk, holding a DualSense, or in front of a classroom teaching Flutter.

I started as a React Native intern in 2021 and never really left the mobile side. Today I sit between the mobile and frontend teams at Azodha — Flutter on one screen, the web app on the other — which mostly means I get to care about how a thing feels on every device it lands on.

I also go back to IMCC, the college where I did my MCA, as a guest lecturer. Teaching Flutter and software development to students who are exactly where I was a few years ago is the fastest way I know to find the holes in my own understanding.

Outside work it’s the same instinct pointed somewhere else: a desk I keep tuning, a PS5 I waited years for, and a camera pointed at both.
