01

Work

Software engineer at Azodha, sitting between the mobile and frontend teams — Flutter on one screen, the web app on the other. Most of my week goes to shipping features that have to feel right on every device they land on, and to the unglamorous work underneath that: state management that doesn’t rot, and screens that stay fast as the app grows.

02

Teaching

Guest lecturer at IMCC, the college where I did my MCA — Flutter and software development, to students who are exactly where I was a few years ago. It stays on this list because it is still the fastest way I know to find the holes in my own understanding.
