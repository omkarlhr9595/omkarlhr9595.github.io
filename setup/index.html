The Desk

It started with one keyboard I couldn’t buy in India. Everything else followed.

Jun 2024

Still missing

01

A friend’s keyboard in 2021, a YouTube review in 2024, and an email nobody expected to be answered.

I got my first job at Azodha in 2024, and the first thing I wanted was a mechanical keyboard. That itch had been there since 2021, when my friend Anurag picked up a Cosmic Byte CB-GK-16 Firefly on a gig we did together.

I was deep in Kreo HIVE reviews when Hardware Mana put the typing sound next to an AULA F75. That was it. One problem: the F75 wasn’t sold in India yet.

So I emailed AULA India and asked. They replied in five minutes with a launch date — 20th June 2024 — and later a PDF price list. ₹6,999.

I ordered it the week it launched, and the rest of the desk grew around it: monitor, arm, light, speakers, mic, and eventually a PS5. Two years later it’s a complete setup. Just a PC left — and RAM prices keep climbing.
